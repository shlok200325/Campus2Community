Democratizing Civic Problem Solving

How Campus2Community Solves Problems

A seamless 9-step bridge connecting everyday citizen grievances with academic research muscle, public funds, and municipal engineering departments.

1 edit_document

1. Citizen Reports Problem

Any resident logs a complaint in under 2 minutes with photos and auto-GPS location via web or offline toll-free helpline.

2 domain_verification

2. Digital Verification

The portal checks geo-boundaries and filters duplicate tickets across identical wards to avoid double-processing.

3 fact_check

3. Admin Scrutiny

Jharkhand Higher Education & Municipal officers classify severity, technical budget feasibility, and priority.

4 local_library

4. University Enrollment

Colleges across Jharkhand receive automated problem notifications. Student & faculty groups apply to solve it.

5 how_to_reg

5. Selection & Grant Match

The best university proposal is selected and linked with government prototype grants and municipal material resources.

6 science

6. Solution Engineering

Student teams engineer the prototype, conduct water/structural tests, and draft implementation roadmaps in their college labs.

7 rule

7. Ground Field Testing

Live on-site trials conducted in the presence of the complainant citizen, Mukhiya, and local Ward Councilor.

8 construction

8. Permanent Deployment

Municipal contractors or local bodies complete permanent civil installations adhering to the student-designed blueprint.

9 verified

9. Verified Resolution

Citizen verifies satisfaction via OTP, NAAC civic credits are awarded to the college team, and ticket is closed.

About Campus2Community Jharkhand

Campus2Community was created to eliminate the structural disconnect between academic engineering laboratories and rural grassroots public suffering. By treating our state’s public grievances as live capstone engineering challenges, we transform university youth into active civic problem solvers while giving citizens rapid, engineered relief.
