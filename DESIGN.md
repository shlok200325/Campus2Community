## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-integrity, institutional digital ecosystem tailored for Indian public service platforms, municipal hackathons, and nationwide citizen-engagement initiatives. It avoids decorative clutter in favor of radical legibility, cultural dignity, and absolute digital accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AAA compliant).

The visual language balances the authority of established civic governance with the agility of modern civic-tech platforms. Key pillars:
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Instills confidence across diverse demographics, from student participants submitting open-source civic solutions to rural citizens applying for regional grants.
- **Radically Accessible & Resilient:** Engineered for low-bandwidth networks, budget smartphones, variable outdoor sunlight, and aging eyes. High visual contrast, generous touch zones, and clear structural hierarchy take precedence over abstract visuals.
- **Culturally Grounded Modernism:** Subtle references to the sovereign tri-color palette reimagined as functional UI surfaces: deep oceanic navy for institutional structure, vibrant forest green for completed milestones and affirmative actions, and warm saffron/ivory for key highlights and badges.
- **Aesthetic Stance:** Corporate Modern infused with civic utilitarianism. Low-elevation layered surfaces, distinct border contrast lines, and zero ambiguity in actionable elements.

## Colors

The palette pairs high-contrast sovereign tones with resilient neutrals designed to withstand heavy sunlight and low-grade mobile displays.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Navy (`#0F3854`):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary navigation, primary call-to-action buttons, high-priority status indicators, and prominent page headers. Evokes integrity, stability, and governmental diligence.
- **Secondary Forest Green (`#1B7D48`):** The affirmative action driver. Represents growth, approval, verified status, active participation, and submission confirmations. Exceeds standard contrast ratios against both light and dark backgrounds.
- **Tertiary Saffron / Deep Ochre (`#E65100`):** The catalytic accent. Reserved for vital notices, active deadlines, pending actions, and urgent civic announcements. Used sparingly to preserve deliberate focal urgency.
- **Warm Ivory Surface (`#FFF9F0`):** An organic, welcoming foundation for banners, informational badges, and secondary light containers that humanizes institutional forms without sacrificing clean contrast.

### Light & Dark Color Schemes

#### Light Mode (Default)
- **Canvas / Background:** `#F8F9FA` with tinted warmth on content sections (`#FFFFFF` surfaces, `#FFF9F0` civic cards).
- **Text on Surfaces:** Primary text `#091E2D` (Navy Charcoal, minimum 14:1 contrast on white), Secondary text `#3D4952`, Tertiary/Muted text `#5A6570`.
- **Card Strokes & Dividers:** High-visibility dividers `#D2DCE4` (never hairline or low-opacity).

#### Dark Mode
- **Canvas / Background:** Deep Slate Ground `#071520`.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Elevated Container `#0D2232`, Overlay Container `#142F44`.
- **Text on Surfaces:** Primary text `#EDF4F9` (13:1 contrast against `#0D2232`), Secondary text `#B0C2CF`, Tertiary/Muted text `#869AA8`.
- **Accent Adjustments:** Secondary shifts to bright verification emerald `#27A863`, Tertiary warm accent shifts to luminous amber `#FF7A29`.
- **Card Strokes:** `#1D3D56`.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs **Noto Sans** for display/headline roles with **Inter** for dense data reading, forms, and interactive labels. 

- **Noto Sans** was chosen because of its universal unicode consistency across modern Indian scripts (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, etc.), enabling effortless localization while maintaining strict aesthetic parity with English.
- **Inter** handles high-information-density UI components, data tables, and input fields with distinct glyph differentiation (such as disambiguated `l`, `1`, and `I`), critical for rural portal users entering Aadhaar numbers, tracking IDs, and contact info.
- Minimum size for core interaction labels is `14px` (`label-md`). Micro-text under `12px` is prohibited anywhere in the interface.
- Paragraphs cap line length at 65 characters to protect horizontal reading tracking on wide screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8-point spatial rhythm** anchored to a fluid responsive grid.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Maximum content canvas constrained to `1280px` for optimal eye travel.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters.

### Accessibility-Driven Touch Spacing
- All primary interactive elements maintain a minimum target zone of **48px x 48px** (extending beyond Apple/Android standard 44px to accommodate older adults and rough field usage).
- Interactive items stacked vertically maintain at least `space-sm` (8px) gap to eliminate mis-taps.
- Content sections use `space-xl` (40px) vertical margins to form unmistakable physical chunks of information.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **structured tonal layering** combined with **subtle civic ambient shadows** and **crisp 1px boundary outlines**. It rejects trendy diffused neon glassmorphism and deep skeuomorphic drop shadows, which reduce contrast under daylight and confuse assistive screen readers.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Light Mode: `#F8F9FA` | Dark Mode: `#071520`. Pure flat background.
2. **Level 1 (Cards, Lists, Panels):** Light Mode: `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid stroke of `#D2DCE4` and shadow `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 56, 84, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 56, 84, 0.04)`. In Dark Mode: Surface `#0D2232` with a 1px stroke of `#1D3D56` and shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Hover, Filter Panels, Sticky Headers):** Light Mode: `#FFFFFF` with shadow `0 8px 20px -3px rgba(15, 56, 84, 0.10), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(15, 56, 84, 0.05)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Citizen Alert Drawers):** Light Mode: `#FFFFFF` with shadow `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(15, 56, 84, 0.16)`. Dark Mode: `#142F44` with shadow `0 20px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

Every elevated card retains its physical 1px border at all times to preserve structural edges regardless of display calibration.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a **moderately rounded geometry (Level 2)** with an exact corner radius of **12px (`0.75rem` / `rounded-lg`)** for cards, dialogue containers, and panels.

- **Buttons & Core Inputs:** Standardized to `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius. This gives actionable controls clean, intentional boundaries distinct from surrounding card envelopes.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Standardized to `12px` (`0.75rem`) corner radius, balancing approachable friendliness with civic professionalism.
- **Pills / Status Chips:** Pill-radius (`9999px`) reserved strictly for status chips, verification badges, and category tags to differentiate non-clickable state tags from rectangular action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Gov Navy (`#0F3854`) background, pure white text, font `label-lg`, `8px` corner radius. Minimum height of `48px` (desktop) and `52px` (mobile) with `1.5rem` horizontal padding. Focus ring: `3px` solid `#1B7D48` offset by `2px`.
- **Secondary / Affirmative Action Button:** Forest Green (`#1B7D48`) background, white text. Applied for primary positive interactions like "Submit Proposal", "Verify Aadhaar", or "Download Certificate".
- **Tertiary / Civic Outline Button:** Transparent background, `2px` solid `#0F3854` border, `#0F3854` bold text. Never hair-thin outlines.

### Cards
- **High-Legibility Civic Card:** Background `#FFFFFF` (Dark: `#0D2232`), `12px` corner radius, `1px` solid `#D2DCE4` border. Padding `space-lg` (24px). Header area features clear separation with an optional top institutional color accent band (`4px` height) denoting category (Green = Active/Approved, Orange = Pending Review, Navy = Official Announcement).

### Input Fields
- **Accessible Citizen Form Field:** Height `52px`. Background `#FFFFFF` (Dark: `#091A27`), border `2px` solid `#8CA0B3`. Hover shifts border to `#0F3854`. Active Focus shifts border to `2px` solid `#0F3854` with a `4px` outer ring of `#E1EBF2`.
- **Labels:** Always placed permanently **above** the field (never floating labels that vanish or reduce legibility). Accompanied by optional regional language subtitles.
- **Validation Messages:** Positioned underneath with explicit iconography: secondary green check for valid, tertiary ochre for required warnings, crimson `#B3261E` for errors with minimum `14px` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height `32px`, pill shape (`9999px`).
  - *Verified / Active:* Background `#E7F5ED`, Text `#1B7D48`, `1px` border `#1B7D48`.
  - *Pending / Timeline Alert:* Background `#FFF2E5`, Text `#E65100`, `1px` border `#E65100`.
  - *Official Department:* Background `#E6EFF5`, Text `#0F3854`, `1px` border `#0F3854`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Touch-Friendly Controls:** Explicit box size of `24px x 24px` within a virtual hit area of `48px x 48px`. Heavy 2px border `#0F3854`. Checked state fills `#0F3854` with a high-contrast white tick mark.
- Grouped choices are rendered as selectable "Tile Cards" rather than raw isolated inputs, ensuring effortless touch targets for mobile citizens.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Horizontal divider lines are explicit `1px` solid `#E0E6ED`. Row height is spaced generously at `60px` minimum. Alternating row zebra strips use `#F8F9FA` to prevent horizontal line slip when reading large record rosters on phone screens.
